# egakareta

egakareta is a **3D rhythm game**. Every level is built around a song, and the
world you travel through may or may not be synchronized to that music. Your goal
is to ride the beat, carve your own path through a hand-built course, and pick up
as much as you can while staying alive.

## The Core Loop

A run is simple to describe and hard to master:

1. A song starts playing. You are a "snake" that can only turn forward and right.
2. You move through a course made of blocks, timed to the music.
3. You collect glowing **gems** scattered along the route.
4. You avoid **hazards** that end the run.
5. When the song ends, your collected score is tallied.

The "draw your line" idea means that, within each level, there is rarely one
single correct path. You choose how to thread between obstacles, where to grab
gems, and when to take risks for a bigger haul.

## Music and Timing

Every level is paired with a specific track. The course is laid out against the
song's tempo, so the placement of platforms, gaps, and pickups lines up with what
you hear.

## The Building Blocks of a Course

The world is constructed from placeable **blocks**. Most are ordinary surfaces
you travel across — grass, dirt, stone, brick, wood, and decorative pieces like
lanterns, fences, roofs, and arches. These form the floors, walls, and scenery
that make each course feel distinct.

The game is built on a constant push-and-pull:

- **Gems** are the score. The more daring your route, the more you can gather.
- **Hazards** punish mistakes instantly. A single mistimed click or a wrong turn
  into spikes, lava, or the wall ends the attempt.

Skilled play is about reading the course ahead and trusting the rhythm.

## Practice and Checkpoints

Because the courses are rhythm-precise and unforgiving, practice is part of the
design. Checkpoint flags let you rehearse a difficult stretch without replaying
the entire song from the top, so you can drill a tricky sequence until it feels
natural.

## Building Your Own Levels

egakareta is a toolbox. The same blocks used in the built-in levels can be placed
freely to design your own levels set to your own music. You decide the tempo, the
layout, where the hazards go, and where the gems reward a clever line. In this way
the game is as much a creative canvas as a challenge: you draw the line, and then
you (or someone else) try to ride it.

## Inspiration

egakareta is inspired by a few different games and genres. We credit:

- Dancing Line: The original.
- Geometry Dash: One input (click), yet a surprisingly deep skill ceiling.
- osu!: Ranking system.
